What happens next?

  1. 1
    Availability check

    We check the approved Conda channels for the exact package and version.

  2. 2
    Build or review

    Eligible packages are queued automatically. Everything else is routed to an administrator.

  3. 3
    Email update

    We notify you when the module is ready or when the team needs more information.
